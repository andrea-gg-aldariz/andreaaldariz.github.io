About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Nebrija University in Madrid, Spain. My research investigates how bilinguals integrate emotional, social, and contextual information during language comprehension.

I am particularly interested in the interaction between language, emotion, and sound during real-time communication. My work combines psycholinguistic and neuroscientific methods, including EEG/ERP, behavioural experimentation, and computational analysis.

Research

  • Bilingualism and second-language processing
  • Language and emotion
  • Social and contextual meaning
  • Psycholinguistics
  • EEG/ERP and cognitive neuroscience
  • Music and language cognition
  • Multilingual communication
