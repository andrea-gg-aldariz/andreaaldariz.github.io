About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Nebrija University in Madrid, Spain examining how bilinguals process emotional, social, and contextual information during language comprehension.

My work combines psycholinguistic methods, including EEG/ERP and behavioural experimentation. I am also interested in the intersection between language and music cognition.

Alongside research, I teach Music Education, supervise undergraduate research.
