About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Nebrija University in Madrid, Spain. My research examines how bilinguals process emotional, social, and contextual information during language comprehension.


My work combines psycholinguistic and neuroscientific methods, including EEG/ERP, behavioural experimentation, and computational analysis. I am also interested in the intersection between language and music cognition.


Alongside my research, I teach Music Education, supervise undergraduate research, and co-organise the Linguistic Break seminar series.
